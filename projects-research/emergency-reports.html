Emergency Reports

CCSVP is working with SirenGPS on the implementation and evaluation of the 911RTA system in multiple school districts in Illinois. The system will take geo-fenced 911 emergency reports and has the ability to push notifications to school officials, School Resource Officers and other public safety stakeholder to improve the speed with which potential school safety threats can be addressed. CCSVP's role is to evaluate the success of the implementation and outcomes of this system. This project is generously supported by a $500,000 annual appropriation by the Illinois legislature.
